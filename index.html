Welcome to Little Sparkles

Every child arrives with their own spark

We believe children thrive when they feel safe, loved and understood. Our setting is built on strong, trusting relationships with families, where every child is treated as an individual — with their own strengths, interests and pace of learning.

To inspire a lifelong love of learning by creating a joyful, inclusive and stimulating environment where every child feels valued and supported.

Our mission
  • Rooted in the EYFS Guided by the Early Years Foundation Stage and evidence-based child development.
  • Partners with parents Working closely with families for consistent support at home and preschool.
  • At their own pace Activities matched to each child’s stage of development — not just their age.
More about our approach
What makes us special

Little things that make a big difference

Each child is unique. Our dedicated team provides personalised attention and age-appropriate activities that promote cognitive, social and emotional development.

Safe, nurturing environment

A warm, home-from-home setting with safeguarding at the heart of everything we do.

Play-based learning

Play is the foundation of all learning — children explore, experiment and follow their curiosity.

Experienced, caring practitioners

A qualified Early Years team, trained in safeguarding and first aid, who get to know every child.

Bright, engaging classrooms

Stimulating, thoughtfully arranged spaces that invite children to explore, create and imagine.

Daily outdoor play

We head outside every day, in all weathers — mud, rain and sunshine included.

Learning through play

From hands-on play to creative expression

Our curriculum encourages children to grow, explore and thrive. We observe, listen and plan meaningful experiences that spark imagination and deepen understanding.

The seven EYFS areas we plan around

  • Communication & language
  • Physical development
  • Personal, social & emotional
  • Literacy
  • Mathematics
  • Understanding the world
  • Expressive arts & design
